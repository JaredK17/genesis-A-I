Brands We Supply

Abrasives · Tapes · Polishes · PPE

3M

Genesis A&I is an authorised 3M distributor. 3M is the world's number one brand, with the most complete, innovative and current range on the market. 3M abrasives last longer and cut fast and evenly, so sanding goes up to 50% faster and material costs drop sharply.

  • Abrasives, pressure-sensitive acrylic tapes and general tapes
  • Compounds, polishes and adhesives
  • Personal protective equipment, tools and equipment
Adhesives · Sealants · Technical Sprays

WEICON

A specialist range of bonding, sealing and maintenance chemistry for assembly, repair and production environments.

  • Adhesives, sealants and fillers
  • Assembly pastes and high-performance greases
  • Technical liquids, technical sprays and cleaning supplies
Spray Guns · Fluid Handling · Service

DeVilbiss

Application equipment for automotive refinishing and production painting, from hand-held guns to automated lines, supported by full service and repair.

  • Manual and automatic spray guns
  • Fluid pumps, accessories and spares
  • Safety and specialised equipment, plus full service and repairs
Automotive Refinish · Coatings

Super Auto Paints

SAP Coatings is a South African manufacturer of automotive refinish paints and coatings, supplying body shops and production lines with colour-matched systems.

  • Basecoats and colour-matched refinish paints
  • Primers, fillers and clear coats
  • Thinners, hardeners and related products
Abrasives · Polishing Systems

Bulldog

Bulldog started out making products for automotive body shops. Its abrasive range has since grown into complete polishing systems for a wide range of manufacturing and refinishing industries.

  • Established with OEMs and their tier-one and tier-two suppliers
  • Highly regarded in the marine industry
  • Complete polishing systems and solutions
Featured Product · Surface Protection

RAPGARD®

A surface protection tape designed to keep lacquered metal automotive surfaces safe during storage and transport.

  • White polyolefin (PO) film
  • Pressure-sensitive, rubber-based adhesive
  • Protects finished vehicles from line to delivery
